I keep returning to the idea that speed is not always the goal. Some tools are slow by design and that slowness is a feature. A fountain pen forces you to think before you commit ink to paper. A film camera forces you to wait before you see the result. These constraints produce better outcomes, not worse.

The best software respects this too. It doesn't rush the user. It provides the right friction at the right moment.
